⏺ I checked D&D Beyond and found three free sets of rules documentation:

  1. Basic Rules (2024): https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules
     - The current core rules, published Sep 1, 2024.
     - Players: how to play, character creation, all 12 classes, backgrounds and
       species, feats, equipment, spells, a rules glossary and a character
       sheet.
     - DMs: running sessions, a toolbox (traps, hazards, travel, curses), and
       magic items A–Z.
     - Monsters: how stat blocks work, plus 300+ monsters.
  2. Basic Rules (2014): https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/basic-rules-2014
     - The older 5e rules: character creation, how to play, magic, DM tools, and
       appendices on conditions, gods, factions and planes.
  3. SRD (System Reference Document): https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd
     - v5.2.1 (May 2025) covers the 2024 rules. It's licensed CC-BY-4.0, which
       can't be revoked, and comes in EN/DE/ES/FR/IT with a guide for converting
       from v5.1.
     - v5.1 covers the 2014 rules under OGL 1.0a and CC-BY-4.0. It's marked
       legacy.
     - The SRD is the free rules text you can reuse to make your own compatible
       products or tools.

  The Player's Handbook, Dungeon Master's Guide and Monster Manual aren't free;
  you have to buy them.

⏺ I built 12 level-1 characters from the D&D Beyond 2014 Basic Rules. I took
  every class feature, spell list, gear option and racial trait from the site's
  own pages, not from memory.

  How I kept them balanced (my choices):
  - Everyone uses the same standard array (15/14/13/12/10/8), so nobody gets a
    better roll.
  - Everyone has max HP and only the starting gear from their class and
    background.
  - Each character's main stat comes out at +3.
  - The site has 12 race/subrace options, and each is used once. Each of the 6
    backgrounds is used twice.
  - Sleep is the strongest level-1 spell, so only the wizard has it.
  - The site has only one subclass per class. Only Cleric (Life), Sorcerer
    (Draconic) and Warlock (Fiend) pick theirs at level 1.
